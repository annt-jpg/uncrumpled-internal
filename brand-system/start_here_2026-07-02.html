If you're making content this week

Your first week.

Read the strategy

Skim the playbook (voice) and How Uncrumpled Becomes Cool (the whole strategy). 20 minutes.

Watch the visuals

Click through the content visuals, especially @goodgirlsnacks and @getgraza, and read "the formula" at the top.

Prove the look first

Follow the Execution page: build the warm-retro filter + Fraunces text preset, grade 3 existing/stock clips, add a line + end-card, send them to us. Do this before anything else.

Then pull ideas

Once the look's locked, grab ideas from the 6-lenses page and start making more.
