Execution · step one

Don't plan a calendar.
Prove the look first.

We know what cool looks like for Uncrumpled. But we've got no on-brand content yet, so there's nothing to schedule. Step one isn't a cadence, it's building the world: take footage we already have (or free stock), put our look on it, and see what Uncrumpled content actually looks like. Then we react and make more.

01 / Start with the look

The first job is a feeling, not a schedule.

Before any rhythm or posting plan, Reina proves the world. Grab an existing clip, apply our filter + typography + green, drop the end-card on, and we look at it together. Once we can see it, we say yes / no / two tweaks, and then we make lots more of what works.

The whole first step

Take what we've got, make it look like Uncrumpled, and put it in front of us. Seeing beats planning.

02 / The look recipe

The three anchors, made concrete.

This is the whole recognisable world (from "How Uncrumpled Becomes Cool"). Apply the same three to every piece of content, no exceptions, that's what makes it ours.

1The filter: warm retro

A warm, slightly faded, sun-soaked film look on every video. In CapCut / VN / InShot: nudge temperature warmer, drop contrast a touch, lift the shadows so blacks go soft, add light grain, a gentle vignette. Save it as one preset and reuse it every time. (The "after" images below use exactly this recipe.)

2Typography + lines

On-screen text in Fraunces (our brand serif), bold or italic, lower third, cream/white. Same font, same spot, every time. The lines we repeat: "less ironing, more living," "sorry Nan," "spray it and go."

3The colour: our green

Our existing brand green is locked. Use it on end-cards, text accents and props. Green + the warm filter = the two things that make a post unmistakably Uncrumpled.

+The end-card

Close every video on the same lockup, on the green: "Uncrumpled. Less ironing, more living." in Fraunces. It trains recognition post after post.

04 / Reina's first task

Prove it on real footage, then we react.

No calendar yet. Just make three on-brand test pieces from footage we already have (or free stock), so we can all see the world and decide.

Save the filter as a preset

Build the warm-retro look once (recipe above) in your editing app and save it, so it's one tap on every clip.

Set the text style

Fraunces, lower third, cream. Save the end-card ("Uncrumpled. Less ironing, more living." on green) as a template.

Grade 3 existing clips

Use whatever we've already got, or grab a few free stock clips (Pexels / Pixabay). Apply the filter, add one of our lines, add the end-card. Don't worry about the idea yet, this is purely to see the look.

Send them to us

We look together and say yes / no / two tweaks. Once the look's locked, we start making lots more, and then a rhythm makes sense.
