The founder story.
Who hates ironing this much?

1 Hook

If you've ever seen Uncrumpled and wondered who hates ironing that much to create a product for it…

2 Who I am

Hi, my name's Ann, I'm the founder of Uncrumpled.

My partner used to head to work in unironed shirts, right up until his work asked him to start ironing them.

No iron, no heat, no white marks.

We've only been open seven months. In that time we've sold out three times, packed our 10,000th order, and put every dollar back into the business to buy our biggest stock order ever.

Concept 17 · what's in the bottle

What's actually
in the bottle. And why us?

Founder, to camera. Glam, warm, proud. The differentiator: why choose Uncrumpled, what CreaseDrop is and how it works, then the risk-free close. Shoot vertical, keep it moving.

Talk to camera Glam · proud Founder · Ann Ends on 30 day risk-free CTA
The script · read as written
1 Hook

We're not the only wrinkle spray company. So why choose us? Why choose Uncrumpled? Let me show you what's actually in the bottle, and how we're different.

2 Most sprays vs us

Most wrinkle sprays are off-the-shelf, white-labelled water with a bit of fragrance. They wet your shirt, the wrinkle sags for a minute, then it comes back.

Not us though. We didn't set out to make a generic version of a wrinkle spray. We did the complete opposite. We formulated Uncrumpled from scratch.

3 The turning point

Partway through formulation, my dad was diagnosed with cancer. It made me start reading labels, and I learnt that everyday ingredients can cause cancer, or be harmful to you and your family. Including some of the ingredients that were originally in our own samples.

I wasn't willing to put that in a bottle. So I set out to create a spray that's safe for you and your family, and that actually works.

4 CreaseDrop + how it works

16 samples later, the result is our proprietary formula, containing our CreaseDrop™ technology. Here's how it works.

A wrinkle is just a bunch of fibres scrunched together, with the fabric holding tight to that bond. When you spray Uncrumpled on, our CreaseDrop formula penetrates those fibres and relaxes them. Then you smooth it out or add a little tension, and the wrinkles fall out. That's something water just can't do.

That's the difference between something engineered and thought through, rather than a generic water in a bottle with a bit of fragrance.

5 The close · CTA

If you want a wrinkle spray that actually works, this is it. We're so confident in our formula that you can try it risk-free for 30 days. If you don't love it, send it back, and we'll refund you.
