Light & composition

Shoot it like real life, on a good day.

Do
  • Natural light, golden hour, soft morning sun
  • Real, lived-in settings, a little bit messy
  • Candid, in-motion, caught-not-posed moments
  • Warm cream and terracotta grade, gentle film grain
  • Linen, cotton, texture you can almost feel
  • Shallow depth of field, phone-real is fine
Don't
  • Cold blue-grey or clinical white tones
  • Sterile studio setups (save that for the villain frame)
  • Over-styled, influencer-perfect, nothing out of place
  • Stiff posed stock-photo smiles
  • Heavy retouching or plastic skin
  • Dark, moody, or high-contrast drama
