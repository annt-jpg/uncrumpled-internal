The five docs.

Each one has a job. The first two are the brand, the next two are the proof and strategy, the last is how to actually do it.

1Content & Brand PlaybookOpen →
The bible. Our north star, voice, the 5 content pillars, the hook → less ironing → more living structure, the idea bank, the lines, and the guardrails.
Use it to: understand who we are and what we make. Read first.
2Brand MoodboardOpen →
How it should look and feel. Palette, type, the mood words, and reference imagery for warm, real, imperfect, sunlit.
Use it to: get the visual feel right before you shoot or edit.
3Brand Feeling ScanOpen →
The strategy. Six brands that turn a boring product into a feeling, broken down across six lenses, with real palettes and examples, then brought home to Uncrumpled.

4Brand Content VisualsOpen →
The same six brands, but you can watch it. Their real posts sorted into the six lenses, click any tile and it plays in-page, plus a "steal for Uncrumpled" block per brand.
Use it to: see the feeling being built, then copy the mechanics.
5The 6 Lenses, our versionOpen →
Each of the six lenses rebuilt for Uncrumpled: what it means for us, and a bank of ready-to-shoot ideas with hook lines.
Use it to: pull content ideas whenever the calendar's blank.

How to actually do it. The content-brief template, a weekly cadence, the community engine SOP, a sourcing checklist, what to track, and a glossary.
Use it to: turn all of the above into posts, every week. This is your daily driver.
If you're making content this week

Your first week.

Skim docs 1 + 2

Get the voice (playbook) and the look (moodboard) in your head. 20 minutes.

Watch doc 4

Click through the content visuals, especially @goodgirlsnacks and @eat_surreal, our two closest models.

Run the sourcing checklist

From the kit: pull 5 camera-roll clips, save 3 trending sounds, DM 2 customers.

Fill 3 briefs

Pick 3 ideas from the 6-lenses page or the playbook idea bank, fill the brief template, run each through the 2-second test.

Post to the cadence

3-4 posts across the week (mostly less-ironing-more-living), repost 1 community clip. Report top + worst at week's end.
