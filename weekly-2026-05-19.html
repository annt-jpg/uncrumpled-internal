V3 · Type B identity hook + product as the answer
2.60x · NEW LANE
"Type B Travellers, this is for us" works because the identity sets up a problem Uncrumpled solves. Identity hook alone doesn't sell spray — the hook has to route to the spray moment. Every new identity hook needs a clear product route.
Brief 4 new identity hooks — each w/ product route written in
"Lies I tell myself as a Type B who thinks she's Type A."→ Voiceover lists lies: "I'll iron tomorrow" / "I'll prep my outfit tonight" / "I'll wake up early to steam this". Each lie cuts to the chaos (pile, crumpled drawer). End on the truth: spray + smooth + walk out. Product = the punchline.
"Type B mornings."→ Show the morning: alarm × 4 snoozes, run to closet, pull shirt out of pile — it's a ball. Spray + smooth + go. Product = the fix that makes Type B mornings work.
"11pm Ann vs 8am Ann."→ 11pm Ann: "I'll lay out my outfit. Iron it. Get ahead." 8am Ann: opens drawer, everything's crumpled. Spray + go. Product = the bridge between aspirational Ann and real Ann.
"Things only Type B travellers understand."→ 5-item list, spray nests at #2. (1) Suitcase = pile not a pack. (2) You spray, you don't iron. (3) You wear what's least wrinkled. (4) Steamers stay in the cupboard. (5) Hotel iron = decoration. Identity hook + product as built-in answer.
