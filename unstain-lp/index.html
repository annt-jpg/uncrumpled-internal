Frequently asked questions.

What stains does it work on?

Unstain is built for food. Sauce, oil, coffee and the rest of lunch, plus most makeup. The full list of what it handles well, and what it does not, gets published here before launch.

Is this just a baby wipe?

No. A baby wipe is mostly water, and water moves a stain around rather than lifting it out. That is why a napkin and a glass of water usually leaves you with a bigger pale ring than the mark you started with.

What fabrics can I use it on?

Everyday machine-washable clothing. Test an inconspicuous spot first on anything you are precious about. We are not recommending it for anything you cannot wash.

Do I still need to wash the garment?

Yes. Unstain is there so you can finish your night without going home. Wash the garment as you normally would afterwards.

Will it get every stain out?

No, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Some things will beat it, especially once they have set. It is made for the accident that just happened, at the table, while you are still out.
