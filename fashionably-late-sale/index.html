What is CreaseDrop™?

Uncrumpled is a one-of-a-kind proprietary formula made to remove creases from your clothes. Our CreaseDrop™ technology removes the wrinkles from your clothes. No iron needed.

When fabric folds, bonds form inside the fibres, and that is what creates creases. CreaseDrop™ reaches those bonds and relaxes them so the creases fall out.

1

Penetrates the fibres

Sinks into the fabric instead of sitting on the surface, reaching the bonds that hold the wrinkle.

2

Relaxes the crease bond

Loosens the bond that forms the crease to allow the crease to drop.

3

The crease drops out

As you shake it or smooth it out, the crease falls out.

4

It washes out

Made to rinse out in the next wash with a vinegar wash, so there is no build-up left on your clothes.
