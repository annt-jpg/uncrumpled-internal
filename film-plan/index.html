Put it back

The concept
Clothes on a rail and every single one of them is creased. Hands slide along, no, no, no, six or so outfits. Stop on one, pull it out, see the creases, sigh, put it back. Concept 5 · New this session

The timed demo, the spray

ProofOne unbroken takeReal stopwatch in frame
The concept
Locked-off single take, no cuts, and the clock is a real device in the frame rather than a graphic added later. Phone propped against something or a laptop open on the bench, stopwatch running. Start it on camera at zero, crumpled shirt, spray, smooth, hold it up. The stopwatch is still going when the shot ends, and whatever the real duration turns out to be is what it says.
Concept 5b · New this session

The timed demo, the iron

ProofOne unbroken takeReal stopwatch in frame
The concept
Exactly the same as Concept 5, with the iron instead of Uncrumpled. Same camera, same light, same stopwatch on the same phone or laptop. Start it before you have touched anything, then go and get the iron, get the board, fill it, wait for it to heat, press it, and put it all away again. The length of the take is the whole point, so it does not get cut down.

Concept 8 · Swipe file 14 Aug · E3 TUSHY

4 reasons why you need Uncrumpled

Three editionsOne setupList format
The concept
One list format, written three times for three different people. The reasons are not interchangeable between editions, which is the whole point: the mum edition talks about everyone else’s clothes, the travel edition talks about the suitcase, the lazy girl edition talks about sleeping in. Reason 4 is a callback to reason 1 in every edition, so the list closes on the same argument it opened with.
The three editions

Lazy girl hackEdition 1 · Jas

  1. Removes all your wrinkles without an iron.
  2. Means you get to sleep in.
  3. You look put together with pretty much 0 effort.
  4. Did we mention it removes all the wrinkles without an iron?

Mum editionEdition 2 · Mia

  1. Removes all the wrinkles from clothes without an iron.
  2. Works on all natural fibres, which means you can use it on your kids’ shirts, your husband’s work shirt or your own clothes.
  3. You look put together with pretty much 0 effort.
  4. Did we mention you can uncrumple everyone’s clothes with just this bottle?

Travel editionEdition 3 · Sienna

  1. Removes all the wrinkles from your clothes without an iron.
  2. Yes, that includes all the inevitable wrinkles that come out of the suitcase.
  3. Because you didn’t spend all that money on the trip just to spend your holiday ironing.
  4. That linen you love because it keeps you cool. You now do not need to iron it.
How to shoot it

One setup, one bottle, one garment per edition. Shoot all three back to back, the only thing that changes is the copy and the garment.

The garment carries the edition: a work shirt for the lazy girl, a pile of mixed clothes for the mum, something out of a suitcase for travel.

Count them on screen, 1 to 4, so the format is readable in silence. Reason 4 lands as a joke, so hold the beat before it.

Reference, the ad this came from
TUSHY

The entire ad is for people with IBS and nobody else is addressed at any point. "On bad flare days I was going up to 10 times per day and toilet paper started feeling like sandpaper." They run two more of these off the same production, one for women on GLP-1s and one static for flare days.

Clusters, body plus hooks

Longest job on the day, so it goes last. Six persona-led clusters. Each one is a single body plus its own set of three to five second openers, so one body gives you up to six ads. Hooks never move between clusters and a body never restates its own hook.
Cluster 1 · body

Gets changed at work

Body script

You go to get changed. You pull your shirt out of your bag and it has that fold line straight across the middle, or it is scrunched to no end.

So now you either wear it like that, or you go looking for an iron that is not going to be there.

And now you are fifteen minutes late on top of it.

(pull the shirt from the bag, spray, smooth, hold it up)

This lives in my bag. Non-toxic, and it washes out in the next wash.

Cluster 5 · body

You think this does not work

Body script

Want proof, in real time? If you are sceptical, watch this.

(start the demo here, and keep talking over it)

When fabric folds, hydrogen bonds form inside the fibres and hold the crease in place.

Our proprietary CreaseDrop technology penetrates those bonds and relaxes them, releasing the fibres so the crease drops out, leaving the fabric Uncrumpled.

All without heat, or that damp, sprayed-on feeling.

(if you are shooting the half and half version: spray the left half only, lay it flat, one unbroken pan across both halves)

It will not do this on polyester, and I would rather tell you now than have you find out.

Cluster 6 · body

Why it exists at all

Body script

I hate ironing.

In my defence, I did try everything else that was already out there first. All of it was useless, or just as much work as ironing, or super expensive.

Then my dad was diagnosed with cancer, and that changed what I was willing to put on my clothes, and on what my family wear.

So I reformulated the whole thing to be non-toxic.

Sixteen versions later I had it. (demo cutaway)

And now we ship all over the world, which still surprises me.
