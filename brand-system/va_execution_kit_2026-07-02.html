The execution kit.

The other docs explain the brand. This one tells you exactly how to turn it into posts, week after week. Start with the brief template, follow the cadence, run the community engine.

01 / Content brief template

Every post starts with this.

Copy this template per video. If you can't fill the hook line, don't shoot it yet. The structure is the tagline: hook → less ironing → more living (the last two can swap order).

the text on screen that stops the scroll. A question, a bold claim, or a "vs" set-up.

the product doing its thing, quick + light. Never a laboured demo.

02 / Weekly cadence

A rhythm, not a blank calendar.

Aim for 3-4 posts a week. Roughly half should be "less ironing, more living" (the selling pillar); mix in mess, a real one, and the odd moment. Repost community 1-2 times a week on top.

Mon
Less ironing, more living
A selling idea: two holidays / ironing math / less-more swap.
Wed
The mess
Type C relatable (mostly founder-made, Ann). Must have a hook + a turn.
Fri
Real one / community
Founder wins-losses, OR a community repost.
Sun (optional)
A moment
The tender/why lane, or a lighter fun post.

The one rule before anything posts

The 2-second test: does the first 2 seconds make someone need to know what happens, or can they nod and scroll? If they can scroll, rewrite the hook. Do not post before it passes.

03 / Community engine

Turn customers into our content (free, on-brand).

This replaces expensive paid UGC. Poppi and Hot Girl Pickles grew on this. Do NOT run follow/tag giveaways, that's not our vibe. Run co-creation, aspiration and reposts.

Make the ask

In captions + stories: "Spray it and show us what you did instead of ironing. Tag @uncrumpled_ and use #lessironingmoreliving."

DM real customers

Each week, DM 2-3 recent buyers (or people who left a review): "loved that you're using it, would you film a quick 10-sec clip of your spray-and-go? We'd feature you."

Get permission, then repost

Save the best clips, reply/DM to confirm we can use it, repost to Stories always + feed for the strongest ones, always credit the creator.

Run co-creation, not giveaways

Polls people love: "which scent should we make next?", "which of these labels?" (Poppi's biggest posts). Plus comment-bait: "tag the friend who still irons."

Cadence

Repost 1-2 community clips a week. Run one co-creation poll every 2-3 weeks.

04 / Weekly sourcing checklist

Never start from a blank camera roll.

Run this every week so there's always footage ready. Raw phone clips beat glossy, that's the brand.

  • Pull 5 "living" clips from the camera roll (trips, dinners, kids, sunsets, nights out).
  • Film 3-5 quick clips during the week's real life (5-10 sec each, no production).
  • Save 3 trending sounds from the reels tab / the watch-list accounts below.
  • DM 2-3 customers for a UGC clip (community engine).
  • Grab 1-2 "villain" clips as needed (a sad ironing board, staged or free stock from Pexels / Pixabay).
  • Note any cultural moment to ride this week (a show, a season, a holiday).

06 / What to track

Know what "working" means.

Likes are the weakest signal. Report weekly: the top post + why you think it worked, and the worst + why.

Chase these
Watch-time / retention (did they finish it?) · Shares / sends · Saves · Comments · Follows from a post.
Don't be fooled by
Likes alone · Views without watch-time · Vanity reach on a post nobody shared or saved.

Weekly report (one paragraph)

Top post + why · worst post + why · what we'll do more of next week · any community clip worth boosting. Keep it short.

07 / Glossary

The words we use.

Type C
Our people: gloriously imperfect, messy-real, would rather live than keep everything tidy. Not a clinical term, a vibe.
The 5 pillars
Mess · Less ironing, more living (the hero) · Real ones · Community · Moments. Every post is one of these.
The structure
Hook → less ironing → more living (the last two swap freely). It's the tagline turned into a video.
The 6 lenses
How we study other brands: Villain · Emotion + community · Guilt → pride · Branding · Build in public · Entertainment.
Villain
Ours is the whole "supposed-to" life, tedious chores, the pressure to be perfect. Ironing is its sharpest symbol.
Build in public
Ann sharing the founder journey (wins, losses, scrappy hacks) so followers feel like insiders. One of our two biggest untapped levers.
08 / Guardrails

Non-negotiables (full list in the playbook).

No em dashes. Use a hyphen with spaces ( - ) or a comma.
Say "Australian-owned," never "Aussie-made" / "made in Australia."
Never promise "perfect" or "professional" finish, and no polyester / deep-crease claims. Don't oversell what the spray does.
Alcohol: cheeky on organic, but for paid Meta ads lean the payoff toward fun-not-booze (reach + targeting throttle otherwise).
Keep the fun warm and inclusive, never smug. (The Poppi vending-machine lesson.) Sentence case, Australian English.
