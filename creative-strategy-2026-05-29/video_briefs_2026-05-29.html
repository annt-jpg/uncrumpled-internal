Video shot-briefs

The motion concepts parked out of the image bank — built for your 2-day filming split. Each is a hook + shot list + audio + length. Voice rules carry over: no "perfect finish", spray + smooth with hands, fabrics cotton/linen/viscose/denim, no I/she AI-VO (real founder voice is fine on-camera).

Core demo concepts (4)

The proven-format motion pieces. These are the workhorses — film several garment variants of each.

V1 · Real-time, no edits

Day 1 soloProven formatAll personas
Opening line (to camera): "Watch this. Real time, no edits, no fast-forward."
Shot list
  1. Founder to camera, holds up a visibly creased shirt (denim or checkered for contrast, not linen)
  2. Single continuous shot: spray evenly, tug + smooth with hands, wrinkles drop on camera
  3. Hold the finished shirt up to lens, beat of silence
  4. End card / spoken: "No iron. No steamer. 60 seconds."
Length: 25–35sAudio: founder voice, no music or low bedCTA: Shop now / link in bio

V2 · Pull-up text reveal

Day 1 soloProven (V7)All
On-screen text rises line-by-line as the shirt smooths: "no iron" → "no steamer" → "60 seconds".
Shot list
  1. Locked-off shot of a crumpled shirt on a hanger
  2. Hands spray + smooth; text animates upward in sync with each smoothing pass
  3. Final frame: smooth shirt + last text line lands
Length: 12–18sAudio: trending-but-calm sound or spray SFXCTA: Shop now

V3 · ASMR

Day 1 soloAll
On-screen: "sound on 🔊". No voiceover.
Shot list
  1. Close macro: the spray mist hiss
  2. Close macro: hands smoothing fabric (mic the fabric sound)
  3. Pull back to reveal the finished smooth garment
Length: 20–30sAudio: crisp diegetic sound only, no musicCTA: soft, end card

V4 · The swish

Day 1 soloAll
On-screen: "Spray. Swish. Done."
Shot list
  1. Hold a creased shirt up
  2. Quick spray, then one satisfying shake/swish
  3. Creases visibly drop out as it settles; freeze on the smooth result
Length: 8–15sAudio: punchy SFX + beat drop on the swishCTA: Shop now

V9 · DM reply

Day 1All
Screen-record a real DM "does it actually work??" then cut to the demo as the reply.
Shot list
  1. DM bubble on screen
  2. Cut to real-time spray + smooth demo
  3. "Every day. No iron."
Length: 15–25sNote: pull seed DMs from the VA's list
Filming-day tip: shoot every demo on a non-linen, high-contrast garment (denim shirt, checkered shirt, structured cotton) — the wrinkle-drop reads far stronger on camera than on linen, which looks flat both before and after. Capture 2–3 garment variants per concept so the editor can A/B hooks on the same footage.
