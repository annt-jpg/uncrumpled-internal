The questions we get in the DMs.

Fair questions.

Does it actually work?
Yes. CreaseDrop reaches the bonds inside the fibres that hold a wrinkle and relaxes them, releasing the crease without heat. It smooths everyday wrinkles well. It will not match the sharp pressed finish of an iron, and we're fine with that.
What fabrics does it work on?
Cotton, linen, rayon, viscose and their blends. It does not work well on polyester or synthetics like nylon and spandex, because they're plastic based and don't absorb the formula. Silk, patch test an inside seam first.
Do I spray it on wet or dry clothes?
Dry. Spray, smooth with your hands, wear. No iron or steamer needed afterwards.
Is it toxic?
No formaldehyde, phthalates, parabens, synthetic dyes or other nasties. We reformulated it 16 times to get it right.
What if I don't like it?
Full refund within 30 days of your product arriving. Email hello@uncrumpled.com.au and we sort it.
Where does it ship from and how fast?
Same business day on orders before 1pm, otherwise next business day, from Laverton North, Victoria. Local pickup available.

How to read this mockup

Inspiration was getdrizzy.co: one product, loud personality, sticker badges, marquee strips, a cheeky vs. comparison, founder up front. We took the structure and the confidence. We did not take the drips, mascots or illustration. Our personality comes from real flash photos of the mess, lime bars and the one-liners.
