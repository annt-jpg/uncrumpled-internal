Shoot 1

The wardrobe pet peeve that started an entire company

Pinned postAnn to cameraPodcast set up
Pillar 2: Unfolding. Instagram + TikTok.
Reference

Seated, mic in hand, talking straight to camera. Big kinetic captions over her, single words punched in. Two minutes long; ours should land well under a minute.

The idea
Ann sits like she is on a podcast and tells the story in one go. Old clips from the journey get cut in between in the edit, so nothing else needs filming for this one.
Script

Ann: If you refuse to touch an iron, this is for you!

Ann: I'm Ann, and I turned my wardrobe pet peeve into a brand with over 20,000 bottles sold across six countries.

Ann: As a working mum, I literally hated ironing. I just wanted to look put together without wasting 20 minutes every morning over an ironing board.

Ann: Now, does it replace ironing? Is it as good as ironing? Does it give you that pressed, crisp look like ironing? No. But it's a quarter of the work to remove those everyday wrinkles and look put together in just seconds.

Ann: And unlike some standard fabric sprays loaded with harsh chemicals, or that are literally just water with fragrance, I made sure ours is made with safe, non-toxic ingredients that actually work. It's powered by our CreaseDrop technology, which gets into the fibres and drops the wrinkles out.

Ann: If you're looking for an ironing alternative for those mornings when you've got one shirt and can't be bothered pulling out the whole board, this might be the product for you.

Ann: To everyone who has purchased, thank you so much for trusting us. What started as a small idea I risked my car deposit on turned into 20,000 of you hating ironing as much as I did.

Ann: If you're ready to break up with your iron, join the club. Tap the link now. And if you don't love it, you've got a money back guarantee.

Shots
  • Ann seated, podcast style.
  • Full script in one take, then again paragraph by paragraph as safety.
  • (edit) Old journey clips cut in between. Pulled from the archive, not filmed today.
Props
Uncrumpled bottle in shot.

I'm not going to lie

Talking headProduct demoHonest hook
Pillar 2: Unfolding. Instagram + TikTok. Talking video.
Reference
No reference on the sheet. Same chair as Shoot 1, so film it straight after.
The idea
Ann opens to camera with the honest line, cut to the demo, then the payoff. The admission is the hook.
Script

Ann: I'm not going to lie to you, this isn't going to give you that pressed, perfectly crisp ironed look.

(cut to the demo. Creases drop.)

Ann: But what it does do is get rid of those everyday wrinkles in your shirts, so you can walk out looking like you've got your life put together.

(alt ending): ...so you can walk out looking like you didn't just pull your clothes out of the bottom of the drawer.

Shots
  • Opening line to camera, same set as Shoot 1. Three or four reads.
  • Usual product demo on a properly creased shirt.
  • Payoff line, both endings.
Props
Same set as Shoot 1. One creased cotton shirt.
