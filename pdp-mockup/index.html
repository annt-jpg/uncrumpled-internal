What is CreaseDrop™?

CreaseDrop™ is our proprietary formula, engineered to make creases fall out of your garments. It's not water and it's not a perfume cover-up - it's the reason a crease drops instead of just getting damp.

1
Penetrates the fibres
Sinks into the fabric instead of sitting on the surface, reaching the bonds that hold the wrinkle.
2
Relaxes the weave
Loosens the tension locking the fold in place - the work an iron does with heat, done cold.
3
The crease drops out
As it dries the fibres settle flat. No heat, no pressing, no sticky residue.
4
It washes out
Engineered to rinse out in the next wash, so there's no build-up left on your clothes.

Everything you'd ask before buying.

What fabrics does it work on?
Linen, cotton, viscose, rayon, cotton/poly blends and most machine-washable fabrics.
What does it NOT work on?
We'd rather be straight with you: it's not designed for leather, suede, silk or "dry clean only" fabrics, and very deep set-in creases may need a second pass. Spot-test anything precious first.
Do I spray on wet or dry clothes?
Dry. Spray over the crumpled garment, smooth, and it dries in seconds.
Do I need to iron after using it?
No. That's the whole point - no iron, no steamer, no heat.
Are there any toxic ingredients?
No. Free from phthalates, formaldehyde and other toxic chemicals. Safe for skin and clothing.
What's the science behind it?
CreaseDrop™ sinks into the fibres, relaxes the weave and lets the crease drop out as it dries - no heat needed. See "What is CreaseDrop?" above.
Does it wash out?
Yes - it rinses clean in the next machine wash, with no build-up on your clothes.
