How the six connect — the formula

These lenses aren't separate boxes, they feed each other. This is the engine behind every brand in here, and behind Uncrumpled.

Villain + Pride → Community
Going against the villain, and being proud you did, is what builds a tribe. Hot Girl Pickles: the boring shelf → "hot girls eat pickles" → the club you want to join.
Emotion + Branding → Community & Entertainment
The branding does the emotional work: a clear identity people want to belong to, shown in fun content. Graza: real people bonding over food. Poppi: "that could be my life."
Build in public → Trust
The founder's journey, shown honestly, adds a layer of trust under everything else.
Entertainment → ties it all together
Makes the whole thing watchable and shareable. Not every post is buzzy (some are just a plain demo), but entertainment is the thread that carries the rest.
The Uncrumpled version

Villain (the iron / the "supposed-to" life) + Pride ("I don't even iron anymore, I got my time back") → the community of non-ironers who live life and don't sweat being perfect.

Emotion (weight off your shoulders, permission to not be perfect) + Branding (less ironing more living, loud, proud, fun) → community + entertainment.

Build in public (Ann's journey) builds trust. Entertainment makes it all watchable.

Who it's for: a mix of Graza (everyone, any age, body, gender) and Poppi (30s-50s, still aspirational), not just Gen Z. We keep Type C, and add the layer of having fun in life while we do it.
