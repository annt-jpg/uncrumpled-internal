Angles — 4

Cut to A·B·C·D. A and B both run per-persona; the difference: A names the lived pain moment (no label), B names the tribe out loud ("Type C, this is you" / "travel hack for Type Cs") then nests the same pain+solve so they self-identify.

A

Pain → Solution

The real pain, per persona — mental load, "need another shirt," "not worth the whole board," "no iron on holiday." Then the 60-second fix. Cold-conversion workhorse.

Open: clothing-specific daily pain is owned by an apparel brand, not a spray. The pain is the friction, not the wrinkle.
B

Aspiration → Identity (callout)

Name the identity directly: "Type C, this is you." / "A travel hack for Type Cs." / "Mum hack." Then the pain + solve for that tribe. They lean in: "omg that's me."

Open: nobody owns the named psychographic. "Type C ___ hack" framing converts the persona's own self-image into the hook.
C

Authority · Social proof · Trust

The human moat. Founder Ann + 15 failed samples + sold out 3× + 110 reviews/4.5★ + (real) press. AND founder-as-one-of-you: "I'm a mum, I travel, I hate ironing too."

Open: competitor is faceless. "Hi, I'm Ann" is already #1. Layer identity into authority.
D

Curiosity → Reveal (demo-led)

Demo is the star. Vary the treatment — pull-up text, real-time no-edit, the swish, ASMR, before/after — and land the after so it self-generates "did you SEE that?!" (which is social proof). Also quietly kills the "doesn't work" objection by showing the tug.

Shared turf: competitor is 100% demo video. Win on no-edit honesty + founder hand + treatment variety, not the demo alone.

Removed this round (per your call)

E (Social proof → Permission) — cut. "You don't need permission." Real social proof (sold out 3×, reviews, press) folded into C. · F (Anti-pattern → Reframe) — cut entirely (steamer/iron-aid reframe). Steamer-faff survives only as a beat inside a demo, not its own angle.
