Why Uncrumpled

CreaseDrop™

Wrinkles aren't on your clothes. They're in them.

CreaseDrop™ is our own formula. We developed it ourselves and reformulated it 16 times to get it right, so it's exclusive to Uncrumpled. You won't find it in any other spray.

1. The crease

When fabric creases, the fibres get crunched together and form hydrogen bonds. Those bonds hold the crease in place.

2. CreaseDrop™

CreaseDrop™ gets into the fibres and relaxes those bonds. A little tension from your hands and the crease drops out.

3. The result

Crease-free clothing, no heat and no damp feeling. It washes out in your next wash.

Fabric guide

What it works on

Works on

Cotton

Linen

Viscose

Rayon

Blends

Silk

Satin

Patch test on an inside seam first.

Not for

Polyester, nylon and other synthetic materials.

Why not?

CreaseDrop™ works by getting into the fibres to release the wrinkle. Polyester is essentially plastic, so it's like spraying a plastic bag. There's nothing for it to get into.
