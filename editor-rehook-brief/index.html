Editor brief · re-hook the winners · 13 to 26 July 2026

Five proven ads. Five new hooks each. Same body, every time.

These five are the best performing cold ads of the last two weeks. The job is not to make new ads. The job is to replace the first two seconds of each one, five different ways, and leave everything after that untouched. Masters are in the masters/ folder next to this page.

Why we are doing this

Already proven in the account

Ads 2 and 3 in this pack are the same footage

Same shoot, same wardrobe, same body, same end card. The only difference is the opening two seconds. One opens on "Travelling soon? We made this for you", the other opens on "Stop scrolling if you're travelling soon".

Both landed in the top four of the account. Neither cannibalised the other. That is the whole argument for this brief: the body is already validated, so a new opener is a new ad at almost no cost and almost no risk.

Ranking below is cold traffic only. Retargeting and warm audiences are excluded because cheap warm audiences inflate returns and hide what is actually working on new people. The same creative running in AU, UK and NZ is counted once as a single ad.

Rules for every edit

Non negotiable
Copy
  • No timing claims. Nothing that says how fast it works. No "in seconds", no "30 seconds", no "instantly". The bottle label says it, our ads do not.
  • No em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.
  • "Aussie owned and operated" only. Never "Aussie made" or "made in Australia".
  • Do not repeat. The new opener must not use the same words as the line that follows it, or as the Meta headline.
  • Sentence case, the way the existing on-screen text already reads.
Edit
  • Change the opener only. Everything after the cut point stays frame for frame, including the end card.
  • Match the existing text style per ad. A1 uses the white sticker, A4 uses green outline, A5 uses green over the product. Do not standardise them.
  • Keep it 9:16 at 1080x1920 or better. Do not crop to square.
  • Keep the captions that are burned into the body.
  • Two seconds is the target for the new opener. Three at the absolute most.

While you are in there, one extra cut per ad

For each of the five ads, also export a version trimmed to 15 seconds or under, using the strongest single demo beat. Our own organic data shows anything at or under 20 seconds gets roughly 3.6 times the reach of a longer cut, so the short versions are likely to outperform, not just add volume. A1 at 55 seconds benefits from this most.
