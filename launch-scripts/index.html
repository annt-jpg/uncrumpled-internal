Uncrumpled · Founder scripts

Uncrumpled goes global 🇦🇺→🇺🇸🇬🇧🇳🇿

Eight scripts. Three for the USA, three for the UK, two for New Zealand. Signature move across all of them: start talking, then cut yourself off with "wait, let me just show you" and do the demo live. USA = we set up our own warehouse. UK = we ship straight from Australia. Tap any script to copy.

⚑ Confirm before you film
  • Sold out four times · 13,000 units in eight months — your own claims, fine to say. Confirm they're current before posting.
  • $35 shipping (US only) — casual story beat, not a hard stat. Good.
  • US3 claims — confirm the warehouse location (Utah?) and the old 2-3 week shipping time before posting.
  • UK ships from Australia — no warehouse beat, and go light on the thick Aussie accent (Brits don't buy into it).
  • Brand-clean: no em dashes, no timing claims, no "made in Australia" (we say "a bit of Australia", never manufactured there).

USA launch (own warehouse)

Three different hooks, same core: real launch story + a live demo. Accent flavour welcome, Americans love it.

Hero · conversational + demo

Hook: founder confession. Open: Australia → USA. Rambly, warm, real. The "wait, let me show you" cut-off is the hook.
tap to copy

Hey USA, we need to talk.

I made something over here in Australia and, not to toot my own horn, but it's amazing. We've sold out four times. We've sold thirteen thousand units in just eight months, and...

[Cut yourself off] Actually, wait. Let me just show you.

[Hold up a crumpled shirt, spray it, smooth it by hand, reveal] This is what it does. Real time.

So, we opened shipping to the US and you blew us away with your orders. The problem was, typical ADHD me, I didn't check the actual shipping costs first. Turns out it's about thirty five dollars to send one order from Australia to the USA.

So I said, what the hell. We moved our stock and set up our own warehouse over in the US. No business plan, no strategy. Just a product we love that we want to push out into the world.

USA, hold onto your cowboy hats. Uncrumpled is coming for you.

End card: Uncrumpled. Coming to the USA.
The self-interruption and the ADHD-me honesty are what make it feel like you, not an ad. Keep it a touch messy on purpose.
