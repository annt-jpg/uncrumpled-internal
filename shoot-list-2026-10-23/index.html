(dream: Ann in a really pretty green dress, uncrumpling the pile with Uncrumpled. Spray. Smooth. Spray. Smooth.)

(wakes up. Bottle of Uncrumpled next to her.)

Shoot 3A

Phone call: friend, brunch catch-up

SkitVideo callHallway
Reference

On the phone, says she is ready, is not.

Friend is a voice on the phone (WhatsApp video call works too), already at the cafe. Ann says she is ready and is not. She sprays the wrinkles out in front of the camera so the friend can see it is done.
Script

Friend (phone): Hey, I just got to the cafe and grabbed us a table.

Ann: Oh!

Friend: Have you left the house? Are you ready?

Ann: Yeah yeah, I'm ready. I just need to get the wrinkles out of my clothes.

Friend: Oh, it's going to take you 20 minutes to iron.

Ann: Iron? No, I literally just spray it. Look, on the video call.

(spray. Smooth. Shows the phone.)

Ann: See? Literally done.

Friend: Oh my god. How did you do that?

(out the door)

Props
Phone (video call), creased shirt, bag, bottle by the door.
Shoot 3B

Phone call: travel (Bali)

SkitTravelHallway
She is on holiday in Bali. Friends are already seated at dinner and she is still getting ready. The travel feel comes from small things in shot: open suitcase, flip flops, straw hat, sarong, passport, a coconut or cocktail. Play restaurant noise and island music through the friend's side of the call.
Script

(on screen: Bali. Dinner at 7.)

Friend (phone): Are you at the restaurant yet? We're all seated.

Ann: Yeah yeah, I'm just leaving the villa now.

(looks down at the shirt. Creased.)

Ann: Yeah yeah, I'm leaving now. I just need to get the wrinkles out of my clothes.

Friend: Huh. That's going to take you forever.

Friend: You always do this when we're on holiday.

Ann: No no, I found a spray this time. I'm literally walking out the door now.

(spray. Smooth. Walks out.)

Props
Phone, creased linen dress, bottle, open suitcase, flip flops, straw hat, sarong, sunglasses, passport, coconut or cocktail.
