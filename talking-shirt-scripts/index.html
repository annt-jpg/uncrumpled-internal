Talking Shirt Ad Scripts

Next batch of AI talking-shirt videos, built off the format that's working. Brief: keep the shirt character, drop the sad/depressing tone, make it fun and upbeat. Three angles below to pick from.

3 concepts ~20–30s each Shirt POV 07 Jul 2026
01
Hype shirt

“Come on, we got this”

Human tired and dreary. Shirt walks her through the morning one small win at a time. Low-key, warm, not a pitch.
~25 sec
Scene. Dim bedroom, early morning. Human slumped on the edge of the bed, tired, hair a mess. Shirt on the chair or hanger, calm and encouraging.
Human
(groan, barely moving)
Shirt
Hey. One step at a time. You've got this.
Shirt
Step one: pick up the bottle, give me a spray, wrinkles disappear in seconds. That's twenty minutes of ironing you just saved.
She sprays + smooths. Wrinkles drop out.
Shirt
Okay, cool. Step two: go get your morning coffee. We both know you're not you until you've had one.
Shirt
And by the time you're back, that's two wins on the board and we are ready to enjoy the day.

Why it works

  • Shirt = the calm, capable one in a chaotic morning. On-brand Type C.
  • Warmth + banter instead of pity. Fixes the “too sad” note.
  • Coffee beat = relatable, gives the fix a real payoff.

Tone

  • Upbeat, best-friend energy
  • Fast, light, a little cheeky
Spray. Smooth. Done.

02
Shirt vs the iron

“Not the iron”

Crumpled shirt on the chair spots the iron coming out and dreads it. 220 degrees, takes forever. Wishes for another way. Uncrumpled shows up.
~25 sec
Scene. Crumpled shirt slumped on the chair, wrinkly, a bit over it. Across the room, the iron gets pulled out.
Shirt
(spots iron, panics) Oh no. No no no. Not the iron.
Cut to the iron. Steaming. Menacing.
Shirt
That thing is 220 degrees. Takes forever. I just want to get out of here and get on with the day. There has to be another way.
Uncrumpled bottle appears in frame.
Shirt
(hopeful) …Wait. Hello.
Spray + smooth. Wrinkles drop. Shirt perks up.
Shirt
Oh, that's the stuff. No heat, no iron, no twenty-minute routine. A few seconds and I'm good to go.
Shirt walks out the door. Passes the iron, throws it a cheeky tongue-poke / “see ya” face on the way past.

Why it works

  • Iron = the villain. Clean us-vs-them the audience already feels.
  • Stakes + relief arc, but played funny, not sad.
  • “220 degrees / takes forever” sells the pain without a lecture.

Tone

  • Comedy dread → relief
  • Iron shot styled a touch dramatic on purpose
Spray. Smooth. Done.

03
Two shirts

“First time out?”

New shirt is scared of the iron. Veteran shirt reassures it: she hates wasting time ironing. Spray, and the new shirt walks off smiling.
~30 sec
Scene. Two shirts in or near the wardrobe. New shirt crumpled and nervous. Veteran shirt smooth, relaxed, seen it all.
New shirt
(nervous) She's got the… is that an iron? I can't do 220 degrees, I just got here.
Veteran
Relax. First time out?
New shirt
That obvious?
Veteran
She doesn't iron. Hates wasting time on it. Watch this.
Human sprays the new shirt + smooths. Wrinkles fall out.
New shirt
(surprised, then grinning) …Oh. That was it? No heat?
Veteran
Few seconds, non-toxic, done. Welcome to the good life, kid.
New shirt beaming, ready to go.

Why it works

  • Mentor / rookie duo carries the info as banter, not a pitch.
  • Sets the scene fast: fear of iron → reassurance → payoff.
  • Ends on a smile. Fully clears the “too sad” note.

Tone

  • Warm, wise-elder comedy
  • New shirt earnest, veteran deadpan
Spray. Smooth. Done.

Production notes

  • No em dashes anywhere in VO or on-screen text (brand scrub rule).
  • Claims kept safe: non-toxic, fragrance-free, no iron, few-seconds. Add “Aussie owned” end-card line if wanted (not “made in Australia”).
  • Each runs ~20–30s. Cut a 60–90s hero + short teaser from the same shoot.
  • Same talking-shirt AI treatment as the current winning video.
  • Pick 1–3 to build into final shot-ready scripts, or tweak lines first.
