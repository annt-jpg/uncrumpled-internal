Uncrumpled · briefs for an AI creator

Films we can make
without a shoot day.

Each one is an idea, a feel and a storyboard. Every frame on these pages was generated, so the film can be judged before anyone is paid to build it. How it moves is up to whoever makes it.

What holds across all of them

  • No dialogue to camera. Every line is spoken over the picture, never written on it.
  • Faces turned away. Keeps real people off the page and dodges the thing AI is worst at.
  • Nobody winks. The mismatch is the joke and the audience does the laughing.
  • Beautiful and expensive. Slower than feels right, macro on everything, clothes treated as valuable.
  • Nothing is ever spilled or stained. A stain on screen wants a fix on screen.
  • Never fake the proof. AI gets the world and the wardrobe. Demos stay real and stay filmed.

Nobody's ironing that

The pleated skirt. Nobody is ironing that.

For Uncrumpled rather than Unstain, and the only idea here that ports to every market for free. Five garments that are objectively a nightmare to iron, shot as extreme macro fabric.
