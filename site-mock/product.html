What is CreaseDrop™?

CreaseDrop™ is our proprietary formula, engineered to make creases fall out of your clothes. When fabric folds, bonds form inside the fibres and that is what creates the creases. CreaseDrop™ reaches those bonds and relaxes them, so the crease drops out.

Will it work on my...

The fabric guide.

We would rather tell you no than have you find out on a Monday.

Yes

  • Cotton
  • Linen. Especially linen. Linen is dramatic.
  • Viscose
  • Rayon
  • Cotton and linen blends
  • Denim, lighter weights

Patch test first

  • Silk. Inside seam, wait, then decide.
  • Wool blends
  • Anything with a print you love

No

  • Polyester
  • Nylon
  • Spandex and activewear

Polyester is plastic. Spraying it is like spraying a plastic bag: CreaseDrop can't get into the fibre to release the wrinkle. The good news is polyester barely crumples in the first place.
