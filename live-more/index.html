What is CreaseDrop™?

Uncrumpled is a one-of-a-kind proprietary formula made to remove creases from your clothes. Our CreaseDrop™ technology removes the wrinkles from your clothes. No iron needed.

When fabric folds, bonds form inside the fibres, and that is what creates creases. CreaseDrop™ reaches those bonds and relaxes them so the creases fall out.

1

Penetrates the fibres

Sinks into the fabric instead of sitting on the surface, reaching the bonds that hold the wrinkle.

2

Relaxes the crease bond

Loosens the bond that forms the crease to allow the crease to drop.

3

The crease drops out

As you shake it or smooth it out, the crease falls out.

4

It washes out

Made to rinse out in the next wash with a vinegar wash, so there is no build-up left on your clothes.

Frequently asked questions.

What fabrics does it work on?

Uncrumpled works on most machine-washable fabrics including cotton, linen, rayon, viscose, and cotton/polyester blends. Test delicate items first.

Do I need to spray on wet clothes?

No. Apply Uncrumpled to dry garments.

Do I need to iron after using it?

No iron or steamer needed. Spray, smooth, and wear.

What fabrics does it not work on?

It does not work well on polyester and synthetic fabrics like nylon or spandex, because they are plastic based and do not absorb the formula.

What is the science behind it?

CreaseDrop reaches the bonds inside the fibres that hold a wrinkle and relaxes them, releasing the crease without heat.

Will it get out deep creases?

It smooths everyday wrinkles well, but it will not match the sharp, pressed finish of ironing.

Are there any toxic ingredients?

No formaldehyde, phthalates, parabens, synthetic dyes, or other nasties. We reformulated it 16 times to get it right.
